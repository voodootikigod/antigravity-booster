Antigravity meters models in independent quota pools. `agb` keeps a semaphore per pool and routes each ticket to the least-loaded pool that can serve its tier, so a run spreads load across pools instead of queueing on one ([lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L1-L5)).

## Two levels of pools

Every model belongs to one **model pool**, and every model pool belongs to one **upstream quota family**. A slot is granted only when both have room ([lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L1590-L1597)).

| Model pool | Models | Upstream family | Default cap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gemini-flash` | `gemini-4-hydrogen-{low,medium,high,xhigh}` | `gemini` | 8 |
| `gemini-pro` | `gemini-4-neon-{low,medium,high,xhigh}`, `gemini-4-carbon-{low,medium,high}`, `gemini-pro-orchestrator-neon`, `gemini-pro-orchestrator` | `gemini` | 4 |
| `claude` | `claude-sonnet-5-5-{low,medium,high}`, `claude-opus-5-5-{low,medium,high}` | `claude_gpt` | 4 |
| `gpt-oss` | *(empty post-Nov-30; retained for custom/legacy compatibility)* | `claude_gpt` | 2 |

Model lists (`MODELS`, `TIER_CANDIDATES`, `PROSECUTORS`, and `PROSECUTOR_CANDIDATES` are centralized in `lib/models.mjs` and re-exported by `lib/pools.mjs` and `lib/agy.mjs`): [lib/agy.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/agy.mjs#L90-L104). Default per-pool caps (`DEFAULT_CAPS`): [lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L71-L76). Family mapping: [lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L769-L773).

| Upstream family (`BASE_CAPS` key) | Matches `/quota` pool | Base cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `gemini` | "Gemini Models" | 12 |
| `claude_gpt` (alias `claude-gpt`) | "Claude and GPT models" / "Claude Models" / "Anthropic Models" (or `0` / `unavailable` on single-group Gemini-only plans) | 4 |

Base family caps: [lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L63-L67).

### Caps scale with live quota

Before planning or running, `agb` probes `agy -p /quota --output-format json` ([lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L1319-L1328)). Each family's cap then scales with the lower of its remaining five-hour and weekly percentages ([lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L724-L726), [lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L735-L742)), and each model pool's cap becomes the smaller of its default cap and its family's scaled cap ([lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L1434-L1438)):

| Remaining quota | Family cap |
| --- | --- |
| 50% or more | base cap |
| 25% to 49% | half the base cap (rounded down) |
| 10% to 24% | 1 |
| under 10% | 0 (dispatch waits for the reset) |

If telemetry is unavailable, `agb plan` and `agb run` stop rather than guess; a failed refresh trips a circuit breaker that suspends dispatch ([lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L1502-L1504)).

## Tiers

A ticket's `tier` (default `mid`) picks a candidate list. Candidates are listed cheapest first and may span pools, which lets the router pick the least-loaded one ([lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L78-L100)).

| Tier | Candidates (`TIER_CANDIDATES`) |
| --- | --- |
| `cheap` | `gemini-4-hydrogen-low`, `gemini-4-hydrogen-medium` |
| `mid` | `gemini-4-hydrogen-high`, `gemini-4-neon-low`, `gemini-4-neon-medium`, `gemini-4-carbon-medium`, `claude-sonnet-5-5-medium` |
| `frontier` | `gemini-pro-orchestrator-neon`, `claude-sonnet-5-5-high`, `gemini-pro-orchestrator`, `gemini-4-neon-high`, `gemini-4-carbon-high`, `claude-opus-5-5-high` |

`gemini-pro-orchestrator-neon` and `gemini-pro-orchestrator` (`Carbon`) sit in `TIER_CANDIDATES.frontier` interleaved with `claude` candidates so frontier runs spread load across `gemini-pro` and `claude` pools; they remain excluded from `cheap`/`mid` builder tiers and all prosecutor/role tables (and never receive `--effort`, as Orchestrator uses a fixed reasoning budget in the CLI). When an operator opts out via `AGB_DISABLE_ORCHESTRATOR=1` or `plan.disableOrchestrator: true`, frontier routing falls back to `FRONTIER_NO_ORCHESTRATOR` (`gemini-4-neon-high`, `claude-sonnet-5-5-high`, `gemini-4-carbon-high`, `claude-opus-5-5-high`). `route()` prefers candidates whose pool and family both have capacity, then picks the one with the lowest reserved-to-cap ratio ([lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L1770-L1790)).

## `pool_hint`

[C4](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/plan.mjs#L317-L318) A ticket may set `pool_hint` to one of `gemini`, `claude`, `claude-gpt`, `gpt-oss` or `auto`. Any other value fails plan validation. The hint filters the tier's candidates by model family ([lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L1307-L1314)):

| `pool_hint` | Keeps candidates whose family is | Tiers with candidates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `auto` or unset | any | all |
| `gemini` | `gemini` | all |
| `claude` | `claude` | `mid`, `frontier` |
| `claude-gpt` | `claude` or `gpt-oss` | `mid`, `frontier` |
| `gpt-oss` | `gpt-oss` | none |

A tier and hint combination that leaves no candidate fails validation with `no model candidates for tier ... with pool_hint ...` ([lib/plan.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/plan.mjs#L319-L320)), so it is caught at plan time instead of mid-run.

## Prosecutor mapping

The prosecutor prefers a different family than the builder whenever cross-family quota is available, and falls back to a distinct fresh-context same-family model (`same-family-fallback` recorded in `.adlc/manifest.jsonl`) when the opposite upstream quota pool has `0` capacity ([lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L102-L108)).

| Builder family | Preferred prosecutor | Cross-family fallbacks | Zero-Claude / Same-family fallbacks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gemini` | `claude-sonnet-5-5-high` | `claude-sonnet-5-5-medium`, `claude-sonnet-5-5-high`, `claude-opus-5-5-high` | `gemini-4-carbon-high` (for Neon), `gemini-4-neon-high` (for Hydrogen/Carbon), `gemini-4-hydrogen-high` |
| `claude` | `gemini-4-neon-high` | `gemini-4-neon-high`, `gemini-4-carbon-high`, `gemini-4-hydrogen-high` | `claude-sonnet-5-5-high` (for Opus), `claude-opus-5-5-high` (for Sonnet) |

[C20](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L104-L107) Gemini builders are prosecuted by Claude 5.5 when `claude` capacity is positive (falling back to a distinct Gemini model on Free/Plus/Pro-Trial or depleted Claude windows); Claude builders are prosecuted by `gemini-4-neon-high` (`gemini-4-carbon-high`, `gemini-4-hydrogen-high`). `prosecutorFor` takes the first cross-family candidate whose pool and family have capacity, then consults `sameFamilyProsecutorsFor` ([lib/pools.mjs](https://github.com/voodootikigod/antigravity-booster/blob/v1.0.0/lib/pools.mjs#L1800-L1825)). See [Prosecution](/docs/concepts/prosecution).
